      About CarePoint

      Healthcare information, made easier to find.

      CarePoint Health is a fictional healthcare brand, created to show how a clinic, hospital, diagnostic centre or pharmacy could present itself online.

      Its focus is simple: make healthcare information and appointment enquiries more accessible. Clear specialties, a searchable directory and a short request form help patients reach the right people without hunting for details.

      Clear information
      Specialties, services and contact details laid out plainly.
      Simple requests
      A short form that asks only what is needed to arrange a call-back.
      Privacy-aware
      No medical details are requested, and nothing is kept in the browser.
      Built to adapt
      Easy to tailor for dermatology, cardiology, neurology and dental clinics, general physicians, multispecialty hospitals, diagnostic laboratories and pharmacies.
