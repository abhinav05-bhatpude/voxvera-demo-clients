Why UrbanKey

Property discovery, made simpler.

This is the focus proposed for the UrbanKey demo brand: an experience built around what buyers and renters actually need to know.

  1. 01

    Clear property information

    Type, location, price, size and amenities are shown up front, so you can compare options without hunting for the basics.

  2. 02

    A convenient discovery experience

    Search by type, location and budget, or browse by category, and open full details in one step.

  3. 03

    Direct enquiry opportunities

    Ask about a property from its listing, through the enquiry form or on WhatsApp, with the property already selected.

  4. 04

    Residential and commercial options

    Apartments, houses, plots and commercial spaces in one place, for buyers, renters and businesses.
