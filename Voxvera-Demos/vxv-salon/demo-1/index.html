About Luxe Studio

Personal style, made with care.

We believe great hair, healthy skin and a confident look are personal. There is no single trend that suits everyone, so we start by listening.

Luxe Studio is a unisex salon and beauty studio designed to feel unhurried. Our team blends technical skill with an honest eye, so every cut, treatment and finish reflects who you are, from a sharp everyday grooming routine to your wedding morning.

Personal style
Looks built around your features, texture and lifestyle, not the latest trend.
Considered grooming
Clean, precise haircuts and beard work for men, with finishing details that last.
Beauty that lasts
Skin-first treatments and makeup designed to look good and feel comfortable.
The experience
A calm space, clear communication and enough time, from the first hello to the last mirror check.
