Why solar

A practical case for clean power.

Solar is not right for every site, but for many businesses it is worth a serious look. These are the general reasons organisations consider it.

  • Renewable electricity

    Panels generate power from sunlight, a resource that does not run out and produces no emissions while generating.

  • Potential long-term energy savings

    Generating power on site can reduce the electricity bought from the grid. The size of any saving depends on the site, usage, tariffs and system design.

  • More control over energy costs

    On-site generation can lessen exposure to some price changes, particularly for facilities that use a lot of power during daylight hours.

  • Support for sustainability goals

    Solar can form part of a wider plan to lower emissions and report progress to customers, staff and investors.

  • Scalable by design

    Systems can start with one roof or section of land and be expanded in phases as needs and budgets allow.

Results vary. No savings or performance outcomes are guaranteed, and a proper site assessment is needed before any figures can be estimated.
